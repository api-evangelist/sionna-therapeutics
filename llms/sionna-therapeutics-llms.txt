# Sionna Therapeutics

> Sionna Therapeutics, Inc. (Nasdaq: SION) is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company in Waltham,
> Massachusetts developing first-in-class small molecules that stabilize nucleotide-binding domain 1
> (NBD1) of the cystic fibrosis transmembrane conductance regulator (CFTR) protein, together with
> complementary CFTR modulators, to restore CFTR function for people living with cystic fibrosis.
